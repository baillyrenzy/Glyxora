Daily Wellness

Products work best alongside good habits. Here are everyday practices that support overall wellbeing.

A day of small habits

Morning

Drink a glass of water, get some daylight and eat a balanced breakfast. Take Glyxora® with a meal if that suits the directions.

Midday

Take a short walk, step away from screens and choose a lunch with plenty of vegetables and protein.

Afternoon

Stretch, breathe deeply for a minute and keep water within reach to avoid the usual slump.

Evening

Dim the lights, put devices away early and keep a regular bedtime to support restful sleep.

Lifestyle pillars

Nutrition

Whole foods, variety and regular meals form the foundation.

Movement

Aim for activity you enjoy and can keep up most days.

Rest

Consistent sleep timing matters as much as sleep length.

Mindfulness

A few quiet minutes daily can help you feel more settled.
